# Space Shooter

Space Shooter is a simple 2D arcade game made with **Python** and **Pygame**.

Your mission is to move your spaceship, shoot meteors, avoid collisions, and get the highest score possible.

---

## 🎮 Features

- Player movement in all directions  
- Shooting lasers  
- Random meteor spawning  
- Score system  
- Background stars  
- Game reset on collision  
- Background music and sound effects  

---

## ▶️ How to Play

- **Arrow Keys** → Move the player  
- **Space** → Shoot  
- Avoid meteors and try to get the highest score!  

---
